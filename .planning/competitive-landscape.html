01 — the market

Brazilian fintechs live with three regulators on the clock.

BACEN oversight forces three obligations into every regulated fintech's calendar:

  • 24-hour cyber-incident notification — Comunicado BACEN 44.323/2024
  • 3 business days ANPD breach notification — LGPD Art. 48 + Res. CD/ANPD 15/2024
  • 90 days of evidence of a working incident-response plan — Res. BCB 85/2021 Art. 6º

Today these are produced by hand — consultants, Word documents, screenshots. The cost of a single Big4 engagement runs R$ 50,000 to R$ 500,000 per cycle, with multi-week turnaround.

Three differentiators an incumbent cannot ship in under six months.

Regulatory primitives as code

Bacen-specific YAML packs, signed with TSA + e-CNPJ, anchored to a Merkle chain. A foreign vendor would have to acquire SafeWeb credentials and ICP-Brasil certificates. They have not.

Sovereign data path
